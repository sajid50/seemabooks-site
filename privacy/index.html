Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This is the privacy policy for the book apps and for Jungle Monster Hunt, made by Seemabooks, LLC and offered on Google Play and the Apple App Store. Quran Words, Bubble Buddies and Puzzle Pals have their own pages, listed at the end.

What we collect

Nothing. Our apps do not ask for, collect or store personal information. There are no accounts and no sign-in. We do not know who uses an app, how often, or what they read.

How the apps work

Each book app holds its book inside the app. The app remembers a few things on your own phone or tablet, such as the page you were on and your reading settings. This stays on the device and is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Purchases

Some apps are paid, and some have a one-time purchase that opens the full book or the full game. There are no subscriptions.

  • Purchases are made through Google Play on Android or the App Store on iPhone and iPad. The store handles the payment. We never see or receive your name, email address or payment details.
  • The app asks the store one question only: whether the purchase has been made on this account.
  • The stores' handling of purchases is covered by Google's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

Advertising

Our apps show no advertising, with one exception. An older free app, Quran Grammar and More, shows ads through Google AdMob. To show ads, Google may collect the device's advertising ID and information about how the ads are used. We do not receive that information. It is covered by Google's privacy policy, and you can reset or delete the advertising ID in your device's settings.

Links

Some apps have links to our other books and apps, or to our websites. A link opens in your browser or in the store, where that site's own privacy policy applies.

Children

The apps collect nothing from anyone, children included.

Sharing and deleting data

We do not share, sell or rent any information, because we do not have any. For the same reason there is nothing on our side to delete. Your purchase history is kept by Google or Apple, in your own account with them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about an app: support@seemabooks.com
