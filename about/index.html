About the author

Mohammed Sajid Khan is a student of Arabic grammar for the sake of understanding the message of Islam and the Holy Qur'an.

He learned the basics of grammar from several teachers and the rest through self-study. By profession he is an engineer, with a Master's degree in Mechanical Engineering. His most popular books are Arabic Grammar for Beginners with Transliteration and Nahw Meer: An English Guide to Arabic Sentences. He has also written Atheism, Polytheism and Islam, Life, Death and Beyond, a Sarf and Nahw analysis of the first fifty ayahs of Surah Al-An'am, and books on the Sunnah in English and Urdu.

May Allah accept this humble effort and reward it as He wills.
